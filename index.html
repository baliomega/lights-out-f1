Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia
Round 16 of 23 at Sepang, 2 to 4 October 2026

A Bahrain Grand Prix, 6,000 km from Bahrain

This race was meant to run under the floodlights at Sakhir on 12 April. It was called off when conflict made the Gulf unsafe to race in, then brought back in July with a new home: Sepang, which last hosted Formula 1 in 2017. Bahrain keeps the name, the trophy and the ticket revenue. Malaysia supplies the track, the heat and the afternoon thunderstorms.

    What Sepang has already seen

    • 1999Sepang's first race. Michael Schumacher, back from a broken leg, waves Eddie Irvine through to win. Both Ferraris are disqualified over their bargeboards, then reinstated on appeal.
    • 2000The season finale. Schumacher wins, Ferrari seals the constructors' title, and the team takes to the podium in red wigs.
    • 2001A cloudburst sends both Ferraris off at the same corner. They rejoin, and Schumacher still leads home a one-two.
    • 2002Ralf Schumacher heads a Williams one-two, with Juan Pablo Montoya recovering from a first-corner clash with Michael.
    • 2003Fernando Alonso takes his first pole on Saturday. Kimi Räikkönen takes his first win on Sunday.
    • 2007Alonso wins on only his second start for McLaren. A rookie called Lewis Hamilton follows him home in second.
    • 2009Rain stops the race after 31 laps and half points are awarded. Räikkönen is filmed eating an ice cream while others wait on the grid.
    • 2012Alonso wins in the wet for Ferrari. Sergio Pérez, in a Sauber, chases him down and finishes 2.2 seconds behind.
    • 2013"Multi 21." Sebastian Vettel ignores the team order and passes Mark Webber for the win.
    • 2015Vettel wins in only his second race for Ferrari, the team's first victory in nearly two years.
    • 2016Hamilton's engine fails while he leads. Daniel Ricciardo heads a Red Bull one-two.
    • 2017Max Verstappen wins the last Malaysian Grand Prix, a day after turning 20.
